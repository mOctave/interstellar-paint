# This file contains the ordinary painting missions offered in human space

mission "Interstellar Paint: Pirate World"
	name "Change Paint"
	description `Walking through the spaceport, you're surprised to find a franchised Galactic Paint Supply store. If you'd like to visit, press "Accept."`
	color selected "paint job: selected"
	color unselected "paint job: unselected"
	color unavailable "paint job: unavailable"
	job
	repeat
	source
		government "Pirate" "Independent"
	to offer
		or
			not "chosen sides"
			has "main plot completed"
	to fail
		has "Fail Paint Change"
	on fail
		clear "Fail Paint Change"
	on accept
		set "Fail Paint Change"
		conversation
			`The paint supplier is located in a seedy back room, lit by a single flickering light bulb. Behind the counter, a decrepit old man - or possibly orangutan - sits, flipping through the kind of magazine typically confiscated by the parents of teenage boys. Hearing you come in, he clears his throat and stares at you with bloodshot eyes.`
			`	"Whaddaya want, kid?" he growls.`
			choice
				`	"I want a fresh coat of paint."`
				`	"Er... nothing, sorry."`
					goto scat

			
			`	"Lesee now, we've got classic blue, we've got three shades of red, and we've got summa that special green-brown stuff rookie mercs like so much."`
			`	Behind him, the six choices on offer are proudly labelled as Merchant Traditional, Pirate Traditional, Pure Red, Faded Red, and Dirty Green.`
			choice
				`	"Can I see the Merchant Traditional?"`
					goto blue
				`	"Can I see the Pirate Traditional?"`
					goto pirate
				`	"Can I see the Pure Red?"`
					goto pure
				`	"Can I see the Faded Red?"`
					goto faded
				`	"Can I see the Dirty Green?"`
					goto green
			

			label blue
			`	A can of blue paint lands on the counter in front of you with a thump. "Ereyago, kid. Basic boring blue for a basic boring - ah, but I shouldn't swear in front of such a delicate little merchant tourist, should I?"`
			scene "paint/Merchant"
			`	"This'll cost you 13000 credits total. Deal?"`
			choice
				`	"Deal."`
					to display
						credits >= 13000
				`	"I'll pay 10000 credits, no more."`
					to display
						credits >= 10000
					goto bargain_blue
				`	"Can I see the Pirate Traditional instead?"`
					goto pirate
				`	"Can I see the Pure Red instead?"`
					goto pure
				`	"Can I see the Faded Red instead?"`
					goto faded
				`	"Can I see the Dirty Green instead?"`
					goto green
				`	"On second thought, I'll pass."`
					goto scat
			
			action
				event "ip_sw_5"
				payment -13000
			`	The unsavory paint vendor lears at you and exchanges your money for a cart full of heavy-duty paint. Back at the <ship>, you carefully apply your new finish until it ends up looking like a perfectly normal merchant ship.`
				decline
			
			label bargain_blue
			branch scat
				random < 30
			
			action
				event "ip_sw_5"
				payment -10000
			`	The man behind the counter agrees far too readily to your counteroffer - even with the cut he must be making an enormous profit. Nonetheless, you hand over your money and soon your ship is painted in the bright blues of an innocuous merchant.`
				decline

			label pirate
			`	The man behind the counter slides you a can of dark red paint. "Well, kid, can't say ye'll last long flyin' these colors. But a brave captain like you might have a chance."`
			scene "paint/Pirate"
			`	"This'll cost you 21000 credits total. It's rather hard to obtain, after all."`
			choice
				`	"Sounds good to me."`
					to display
						credits >= 21000
				`	"That's a rip-off. I'm not paying more than 15000 credits."`
					to display
						credits >= 15000
					goto bargain_pirate
				`	"Can I see the Merchant Traditional instead?"`
					goto blue
				`	"Can I see the Pure Red instead?"`
					goto pure
				`	"Can I see the Faded Red instead?"`
					goto faded
				`	"Can I see the Dirty Green instead?"`
					goto green
				`	"On second thought, I'll pass."`
					goto scat
			
			action
				event "ip_sw_6"
				payment -21000
			`	The paint salesman grins and starts passing you paint cans, throwing in a complementary paint roller that probably cost about 3 credits to manufacture. Back at the <ship>, you carefully apply your new finish until it ends up looking like every other pirate ship around.`
				decline

			label bargain_pirate
			branch scat
				random < 40

			action
				event "ip_sw_5"
				payment -15000
			`	The man behind the counter agrees far too readily to your counteroffer - even with the cut he must be making an enormous profit. Nonetheless, you hand over your money and soon your ship is painted in the dark reds of a daring pirate.`
				decline

			label pure
			`	The man behind the counter gestures to the bright red paint on the shelf behind him. "'ere it is. Ye want it? It'll cost you, that's for sure."`
			scene "paint/Pure Red"
			`	"Gimme 26000 credits and I'll give you enough of this stuff to paint your ship."`
			choice
				`	(Pay him.)`
					to display
						credits >= 26000
				`	"No way. I'll pay 17000 credits."`
					to display
						credits >= 17000
					goto bargain_pure
				`	"Can I see the Merchant Traditional instead?"`
					goto blue
				`	"Can I see the Pirate Traditional instead?"`
					goto pirate
				`	"Can I see the Faded Red instead?"`
					goto faded
				`	"Can I see the Dirty Green instead?"`
					goto green
				`	"On second thought, I'll pass."`
					goto scat
			
			action
				event "ip_sw_7"
				payment -26000
			`	Surprisingly, the unsavory character is generous enough to help you carry your newly bought paint back to your ship, where you layer on coat after coat and end up with a bright red finish. This ought to catch the Navy's eye!`
				decline

			label bargain_pure
			branch scat
				random < 50

			action
				event "ip_sw_7"
				payment -17000
			`	The man behind the counter agrees far too readily to your counteroffer - even with the cut he must be making an enormous profit. Nonetheless, you hand over your money and soon your ship is painted in the bright reds of a bleeding salamander.`
				decline

			label faded
			`	The man behind rolls a small can of red paint across the counter to you. "You shouldn't need much of this stuff, but it ain't cheap."`
			scene "paint/Faded Red"
			`	"I'll give you 22000 credits and that's cutting my own throat."`
			choice
				`	"22000 credits will do just fine."`
					to display
						credits >= 22000
				`	"Cutting your own throat with credit chips, maybe. I'll give you 16000 and not a credit more."`
					to display
						credits >= 16000
					goto bargain_faded
				`	"Can I see the Merchant Traditional instead?"`
					goto blue
				`	"Can I see the Pirate Traditional instead?"`
					goto pirate
				`	"Can I see the Pure Red instead?"`
					goto pure
				`	"Can I see the Dirty Green instead?"`
					goto green
				`	"On second thought, I'll pass."`
					goto scat
			
			action
				event "ip_sw_8"
				payment -22000
			`	With instructions to layer the paint lightly, the salesman hands you a basket of paint cans light enough to carry back to the <ship> without difficulty. A couple hours later, you end up with a faded red paint job.`
				decline

			label bargain_faded
			branch scat
				random < 40

			action
				event "ip_sw_8"
				payment -16000
			`	The man behind the counter agrees far too readily to your counteroffer - even with the cut he must be making an enormous profit. Nonetheless, you hand over your money and soon your ship is painted in the faded reds of an embarassed sheep.`
				decline

			label green
			branch green_failure
				"combat rating" < 148
			
			`	"Ah, so yer one of those rookie mercs then, I see," the scuzzy storekeeper says as he tosses you a can of paint.`
			scene "paint/Yellow Inversion"
			`	"For most folk, this stuff would cost about 40 grand. For you, I'll do 30."`
			choice
				`	(Pay him 30000 credits.)`
					to display
						credits >= 30000
				`	"I'll pay 20000."`
					to display
						credits >= 20000
					goto bargain_green
				`	"Can I see the Merchant Traditional instead?"`
					goto blue
				`	"Can I see the Pirate Traditional instead?"`
					goto pirate
				`	"Can I see the Pure Red instead?"`
					goto pure
				`	"Can I see the Faded Red instead?"`
					goto faded
				`	"On second thought, I'll pass."`
					goto scat
			
			action
				event "ip_sw_yellow_inv"
				payment -30000
			`	The man behind the counter saddles you with enough green paint to put an algae bloom to shame. After a couple trips back to your ship and a few hours with a brush and roller, the <ship> is decked out with a dirty green paint job.`
				decline

			label bargain_green
			branch scat
				random < 60

			action
				event "ip_sw_yellow_inv"
				payment -20000
			`	The man behind the counter agrees far too readily to your counteroffer - even with the cut he must be making an enormous profit. Nonetheless, you hand over your money and soon your ship is painted in the muddy green of an army surplus cow pasture.`
				decline

			label green_failure
			`	The man laughs heartily. "Oh, you kids make know how to make an old man laugh, donya? Even rookie mercs have more experience then you. Come back after you've shot up a few ships and maybe we'll talk. But for now -" he pauses, handing you a can of merchant-blue paint "- I think this is what yer lookin' for. On the house."`

			action
				event "ip_sw_5"
			
			`	Abashed, you return to your ship to find that someone else has just applied a fresh coat of blue paint to it. It looks like you're not getting your warpaint today after all.`
				decline

			
			label scat
			`	"Whydonya scat, then?" he replies, turning back to his book.`
			`	After one more glance at the dump of a store, you leave.`
				decline
